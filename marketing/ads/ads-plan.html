01Goal

More qualified leads for new garage doors and replacements, at a falling cost per qualified lead, across Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Lee and Monroe counties, residential and commercial.

Repair and service leads are welcome but the campaigns are not built to grow them. Results are judged on leads the team marks qualified, not on Google's conversion count.

05Strategy

One Search campaign, built for sales

  • "305 DOORS | Search | New & Replacement": Google Search only, phrase and exact match, so every search we pay for is visible and controllable.
  • Four ad groups, each landing on its own page: Installation & Replacement, Impact & Hurricane, Commercial, and Local Company.
  • Repair is excluded with negative keywords from day one, along with parts, openers, DIY, big-box stores, windows and entry doors, and jobs. These took most of the 2024 spend.
  • Bidding: Maximize Clicks with a $25 maximum per click while there is no conversion data; switch to Maximize Conversions after about 15 real leads in 30 days.
  • Budget: $32 a day (about $973 a month) against a hard cap of $1,100 a month.
  • Ads: two responsive search ads per group stating free estimates, most brands installed, fast installation and hurricane-approved impact doors, all confirmed by Osniel. No phone number in ad text (Google policy); calls come through the call button. Experience reads "Over 10 Years of Experience" and "In Business Since 2019" (founded October 4, 2019, plus 4 years with other companies). No warranty is mentioned. No financing is offered or mentioned.
  • Calls: the call button shows Mon–Sat, 8am–6pm Miami time; outside those hours ads send people to the quote form.

County by county

  1. Weeks 1–4: Miami-Dade and Broward, the largest demand and closest to the team.
  2. Then Palm Beach, once the first two have a known cost per qualified lead.
  3. Lee and Monroe as small separate tests. Collier stays out of ads; its page stays up for organic search.

Language

Spanish-speaking users are targeted with English ads. Spanish garage-door searches are small and mostly price browsing, so a Spanish ad group waits until search-term data shows demand worth one. The team answers calls in Spanish.

Later

  • A small "styles" test (impact glass, modern, insulated, brands) where clicks are cheap.
  • Performance Max only after about 30 days of real lead data.
  • Offline conversions: send "qualified" and "won" back to Google by click ID so bidding learns which clicks become jobs.

06Measuring success

A qualified lead is a homeowner, property manager or business in a served county asking for a quote on a new door, a replacement or a commercial installation. Repair requests count as qualified but secondary. Spam, vendors, job seekers, parts or DIY questions and out-of-area requests do not.
