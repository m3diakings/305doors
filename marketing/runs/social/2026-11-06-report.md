## Google Business Profile
Copy: Seeing brown streaks or orange spots on a garage door in Miami? Warm, wet air, sprinkler spray and salt in the air near the bay and the ocean make metal hardware age quickly, so rust is common here. The question is whether it is cosmetic or a warning sign.

Light rust on a hinge, bracket or track edge that wipes off with a rag is usually minor. Rust on a spring or cable, flaking or pitted metal, or a door that binds or jerks should be looked at by a trained technician, because those parts carry the door's weight under tension.

A simple habit helps in between visits: rinse the door and tracks with fresh water, dry the bottom edge, touch up scratches with paint made for metal, and keep sprinklers off the door.
